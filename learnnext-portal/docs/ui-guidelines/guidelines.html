01 Principles
01
Principles

Five rules settle every design decision. When two options both look fine, the one that serves these better wins.

01
Calm over loud
Colour and motion carry meaning, never decoration.
02
Clear at a glance
The key number or action reads in under three seconds.
03
Consistent
One component per pattern; light and dark look like one product.
04
No dead ends
Every state offers a next step.
05
Accessible
WCAG 2.1 AA is the minimum.
